About the platform

Built for the two halves of every farm

Most farms run on two parallel tracks — what's growing in the field, and what's living in the barn. Agriculture Supporter treats both as first-class citizens: structured guidance for crops from sowing to harvest, and for animals from housing to breeding, backed by a real database instead of scattered notes.

  • Category-first navigation — browse the way you already think about your farm.
  • Location-aware recommendations tailored to Indian states and climates.
  • An in-app assistant for quick questions, any time you're logged in.
